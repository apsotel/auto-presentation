# Quality gates

## Outline

- Core message, audience, and intended action are explicit.
- Each scene has one conclusion and timing fits the target.
- Unstable claims have sources.
- Dense processes are split into readable scenes.

## Draft

- Opening establishes relevance quickly.
- Visual hierarchy is readable at 1080p.
- Animation supports narration rather than merely swapping text.
- Loops have no flash at the seam.
- Footage shows only relevant windows.
- Captions fit safe margins and remain until narration advances.
- Step highlights follow the spoken sequence.

## TTS

- Voice use is authorized and the reference is clean enough.
- Names, numbers, English terms, and domain vocabulary are acceptable.
- One audio file exists per scene.
- Durations come from ffprobe.
- Captions track narration without repetition or premature fading.

## Final render

- Record duration, FPS, resolution, codecs, channels, and size.
- Inspect opening, closing, boundaries, and difficult footage.
- No black or corrupted frames exist outside intentional transitions.
- Audio is aligned and free from clipping or obvious gaps.
- The file opens and seeks correctly.
- The final filename is unambiguous.
- Deliver a clickable path and summarize changes from the approved draft.
